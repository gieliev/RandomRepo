# Advanced 10KM Training Program

**Goal**: Achieve running 10 kilometers non-stop by **December 31, 2026**.  
**Demographics**: 24-year-old woman, 63kg, 1.73m.  
**Program Duration**: January - December 2026 (12 months).

This program is divided into quarterly phases—each with a specific objective to build you up to 10 kilometers.

---

### **Phase 1: Build Base Fitness (January - March 2026)**  
**Objective**: Establish a consistent running habit and improve aerobic capacity without overtraining.  

#### Training Plan:
- **Weekly Runs (3x per week):**
  - **Week 1-4:** Run 1-2 minutes, walk 2-3 minutes (repeat for 20-25 minutes).  
  - **Week 5-8:** Run 3 minutes, walk 1 minute (repeat for 25-30 minutes).  
  - **Week 9-12:** Gradually extend continuous running time by 2 minutes each week. By Week 12, aim for **15-20 minutes of non-stop running**.
- **Long Runs (1x per week):** Start with brisk walks or easy jogs (20-30 minutes). Gradually increase by 3-5 minutes weekly.

#### Key Focus:
- **Intensity:** Maintain an "easy" pace—use the **talk test**: you should be able to carry on a conversation while running.
- **Consistency:** Aim for 3 running sessions per week and **1 cross-training day** (e.g., cycling, swimming, or brisk walking for 30 minutes).

#### Extras:
- **Develop Habits:** Track progress (use Strava or a fitness app), plan routes to keep runs varied, and focus on building confidence.
- **Strength Training (see below):** 2x per week.
- **Stretching Routine (see below):** After every run.

---

### **Phase 2: Build Stamina and Distance (April - June 2026)**  
**Objective**: Increase running time and prepare your body to run 5 kilometers.  

#### Training Plan:
- **Weekly Runs (4x per week):**
  - **Short Maintenance Runs (2x/week):** 20-30 minutes of non-stop running.
  - **Moderate Distance Run (1x/week):** Start with 3km, increase by 0.5km weekly until you can run **5km by Month 6**.
  - **Tempo Run (1x every 2 weeks):** Run slightly faster than your comfort pace for 5 minutes, walk for 2 minutes, and repeat 3-4 times.
- **Long Runs (1x per week):** Start at 3-4km, increasing to 6km by the end of Month 6. Run at a slow, steady pace.

#### Key Focus:
- **Progress Gradually:** Increase weekly mileage by no more than 10% to avoid injuries.
- **Cross-Training:** Add activities like cycling or swimming on rest days to maintain fitness without overloading running muscles.
- **Listen to Your Body:** Prioritize rest if you feel fatigued.

#### Extras:
- **Strength Training (see below):** Continue 2x per week.
- **Stretching Routine (see below):** Include dynamic warmups before runs.

---

### **Phase 3: Advanced Distance Training (July - September 2026)**  
**Objective**: Build endurance and prepare your body to cover 7-8 kilometers.  

#### Training Plan:
- **Weekly Runs (4-5x per week):**
  - **Short Runs (2x/week):** 4-5km at an easy pace. These are for maintenance.
  - **Moderate Runs (1x/week):** Start with 5km and gradually build to 6-7km over the three months.
  - **Long Runs (1x/week):** Begin at 6km in July, progressively increasing by 0.5km every 2 weeks. Aim to reach 8km by September.
  - **Speed Work (1x/week):** Do interval training: 
    - Example: Run hard for 1 minute, jog for 2 minutes (repeat 6-8 times).

#### Key Focus:
- Include **hilly terrain** in long runs to build strength.
- Focus on efficient breathing and running form—relax your shoulders and keep a steady cadence (aim for 170-180 steps per minute).

---

### **Phase 4: Hit 10KM (October - December 2026)**  
**Objective**: Successfully reach the 10km milestone!  

#### Training Plan:
- **Weekly Runs (5x per week):**
  - **Short Runs (2x/week):** 5-6km at a slow pace.
  - **Moderate Runs (1x/week):** 6-7km, moderate effort.
  - **Long Runs (1x/week):** Progress to the 10km distance: 
    - Start at 8km in October and increase by 0.5km weekly. Run the full 10km by December.
  - **Tempo Runs (1x/week):** Alternate between: 
    - 10-minute warmup, then 4-5km at a comfortably hard pace. 
    - Intervals: 3 minutes hard, 2 minutes easy (repeat 6 times).
- **Optional Recovery Run:** Slow jog or brisk walk for 20-30 minutes on a rest day.

#### Race-Day Tips:
- Simulate race conditions in November (hydration, clothing, pacing).
- Practice running "negative splits" (start slow, finish strong).

---

### **Stretching Routine (Dynamic Warm-Up and Post-Run Cool-Down)**
**Before Runs:** Dynamic stretches to activate muscles (5-7 minutes):
- Leg Swings (front/back and side-to-side).
- High Knees.
- Butt Kicks.
- Walking Lunges.

**After Runs:** Static stretches to improve flexibility (10-12 minutes):
- **Hamstring Stretch:** Extend one leg, reach toward your toes.
- **Quadriceps Stretch:** Pull one foot toward your glutes.
- **Calf Stretch:** Press one heel into the floor.
- **Hip Flexor Stretch:** Lunge forward, push hips down.
- **Seated Twist:** Sit cross-legged, and rotate your torso.

---

### **Strength Training (2x per Week)**  
**Goal**: Improve running power and reduce injury risk.

**Workout Plan (2-3 sets of 10-12 reps each):**
1. **Bodyweight Squats:** Strengthen thighs and glutes.
2. **Lunges:** Forward or walking lunges to target quads and hamstrings.
3. **Calf Raises:** Strengthen lower legs and improve ankle stability.
4. **Push-Ups:** Core and upper body strength.
5. **Plank (30-60 sec):** Increases core stability for better running posture.
6. **Side Plank (30-45 sec per side):** Targets obliques for rotational stability.

---

### **Nutrition Guidelines**
#### Daily Nutrition:
- **Carbs (50% of calories):**
  - Whole grains like oats, rice, and sweet potatoes.
- **Protein (20-25% of calories):**
  - Lean meats, fish, eggs, tofu, or beans.
- **Fats (20-30% of calories):**
  - Avocado, nuts, olive oil.
- **Hydration:** 2-3 liters/day.

#### Pre-Run Fuel (1-2 Hours Beforehand):
- Small meal/snack: Toast with peanut butter, a banana, or yogurt with granola.

#### During Long Runs (>60 Minutes):
- Consider carrying water or an electrolyte drink.
- Light snack (e.g., energy gel).

#### Post-Run Recovery (Within 30 Minutes):
- Combo of carbs & protein:
  - Protein smoothie with fruit.
  - Chicken sandwich or eggs and toast.
