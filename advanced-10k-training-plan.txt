# Advanced 10K Training Plan

## Phase 1: Base Building
- **Duration**: 4 weeks
    - Weekly mileage increases gradually by 10%.
    - Focus on easy runs and proper form.

### Weekly Breakdown
- **Monday**: Light cross-training or yoga.
- **Tuesday**: Easy 5K run.
- **Wednesday**: Strength training.
- **Thursday**: Easy 6K run.
- **Friday**: Rest.
- **Saturday**: Long run (start at 8K, increase by 1K weekly).
- **Sunday**: Rest.

## Phase 2: Speed Training
- **Duration**: 6 weeks
    - Incorporate interval and tempo runs.
    - Hill runs for strength preparation.

### Weekly Breakdown
- **Monday**: Light cross-training or yoga.
- **Tuesday**: Interval training (e.g., 5x400m at 5K pace).
- **Wednesday**: Easy 4-5K recovery run.
- **Thursday**: Tempo run (6K at 10-15 sec slower than 10K pace).
- **Friday**: Strength training.
- **Saturday**: Long run (10-12K with last 3K at goal race pace).
- **Sunday**: Rest.

## Phase 3: Peak Training and Tapering
- **Duration**: Final 4 weeks before the race.
    - Gradually reduce mileage to ensure peak form.
    - Maintain speed with shorter workouts.

### Weekly Breakdown
- **Monday**: Light cross-training or yoga.
- **Tuesday**: Short intervals (e.g., 8x200m).
- **Wednesday**: Medium run (7K at an easy pace).
- **Thursday**: Tempo run (5K at race pace).
- **Friday**: Rest.
- **Saturday**: Long run (taper to 10K, 8K, and 6K weekly).
- **Sunday**: Rest.

## Notes
- Always integrate warm-ups and cool-downs before and after runs.
- Prioritize hydration and nutrition during training.
- Consult with a coach or physician if experiencing signs of overtraining.

---

*Designed to help ambitious runners achieve their best 10K performance while minimizing injury risk.*
